# Mining and ores (Terraria 1.4.4.9)

## Tool tiers

Pickaxes mine blocks and furniture, but not background walls (hammers)
or trees (axes). Effectiveness depends on **pickaxe power** and **mining
speed**: higher power deals more mining damage per hit; lower
mining-speed values produce hits more frequently. Use time is the swing
animation, not mining-hit frequency.

Most early ores (Copper/Tin through Gold/Platinum) have no special
high-tier power requirement. Drills generally mine faster than same-tier
pickaxes, but have shorter range and do not benefit from mining-speed
bonuses.

## Where ores generate

Ores usually appear as veins in the Underground layer and below.
Lower-tier ores can also appear in surface dirt and Floating Islands.
Exact distribution depends on world size and seed.

### Pre-Hardmode

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ore                                 Main generation / source
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Copper / Tin                        Surface, Underground and Cavern

  Iron / Lead                         Surface, Underground and Cavern

  Silver / Tungsten                   Underground, Cavern and Floating
                                      Islands; rarely near surface

  Gold / Platinum                     Mainly Cavern and Space

  Demonite / Crimtane                 Corruption / Crimson; also dropped
                                      by related bosses

  Meteorite                           Meteor crash sites

  Obsidian                            Forms where water contacts lava

  Hellstone                           Underworld
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

A normal world generally generates one variant from each of the first
four pairs and one world-evil ore (Demonite or Crimtane). Fishing crates
and the Extractinator can provide alternate variants. Special seeds may
change these rules.

### Hardmode

After the Wall of Flesh is defeated, the world enters Hardmode. Breaking
Demon or Crimson Altars with the Pwnhammer or a stronger hammer
generates three tiers of Hardmode ores.

  Tier   World variants          First pickaxe tier
  ------ ----------------------- ----------------------
  1      Cobalt / Palladium      Molten
  2      Mythril / Orichalcum    Cobalt / Palladium
  3      Adamantite / Titanium   Mythril / Orichalcum

The world selects one variant from each pair when the ores are
generated. Further altar destruction generates more of the selected
ores. Drunk worlds can generate both variants of each tier.

**Chlorophyte** grows in mud in the Underground Jungle during Hardmode
and slowly spreads into nearby eligible mud. It requires 200% pickaxe
power.

**Luminite** is dropped by the Moon Lord; it is not a naturally
generated underground vein.

## Ore drops and alternate sources

Most mined ore blocks drop their corresponding ore items, which are
smelted into bars at a Furnace. Recipes and ore-to-bar ratios vary by
material. Unlike Minecraft, vanilla Terraria has no Fortune or Silk
Touch tool enchantments.

The **Extractinator** converts Silt, Slush and Desert Fossils into ores,
gems, coins and other resources. Fishing crates can also contain ores
and bars, including alternate world variants. Some ores are also dropped
by bosses.

## Mining speed and useful items

-   **Mining Potion**: +25% pickaxe mining speed for 10 minutes; does
    not increase drill speed.
-   **Spelunker Potion**: highlights nearby ores, gems, chests, Life
    Crystals and other valuables for 5 minutes. Meteorite, Crimtane,
    Obsidian and Hellstone are notable exceptions.
-   **Mining armor** and selected accessories / buffs increase mining
    speed.
-   The **Metal Detector** and its upgrades detect most nearby ores;
    Hellstone and Obsidian are exceptions.
-   Pickaxe power and mining speed are separate stats: a fast tool may
    lack the power to mine a block, while a high-power tool is not
    necessarily the fastest.

## Underground hazards and resources

-   **Lava**: common in the Underworld and underground pools. Mining
    Hellstone releases lava; Hellstone and Meteorite inflict
    fire-related debuffs on contact without suitable protection.
-   **Water and Honey**: pools and pockets occur in caves; breaking into
    one can flood a passage.
-   **Sand, Silt and Slush**: gravity-affected blocks can fall when
    their support is removed.
-   **Traps**: caves and underground cabins can contain pressure plates,
    darts, boulders and explosives. Dangersense Potion highlights many
    traps and also reveals Hellstone.
-   **Enemies**: danger varies by layer and biome; the Underground
    Jungle, Corruption, Crimson and Underworld are more hazardous than
    ordinary caves.
-   **Gems, Life Crystals and chests**: common exploration finds in
    Underground and Cavern areas.
-   **Abandoned Minecart Tracks**: reveal long cave routes; minecart
    chests contain useful loot.
